# Artwork and provenance

## Manuscript figures

The website's 17 research figures are the authors' original manuscript artwork,
published here with the authors' authorization. They are converted to lossless
WebP without changing labels, colors, or scientific content. PDF pages are
rendered at 2600 pixels wide; source PNG illustrations retain their dimensions.

The exact source filenames and output dimensions are recorded in
[`figures/manifest.json`](figures/manifest.json). The active timeline source is
`F02_field_timeline-5.pdf`, not an earlier similarly named revision.

The inline world-model taxonomy hides only blank PDF page margins through CSS;
the figure viewer opens the complete, unchanged image. The social preview PNG
is an earlier web rendering of the same active overview figure.

These are explanatory illustrations from the survey, not newly generated model
outputs or experimental results. Third-party research depicted in the figures
remains credited in the manuscript and the reference collection.

## Operation symbols

The paper uses Font Awesome's eye (`f06e`), pencil (`f040`), exchange (`f0ec`),
and gamepad (`f11b`) glyphs on colored discs. The SVGs in `icons/` use those same
glyph outlines, extracted from the official
[Font Awesome 4.7.0 font](https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/blob/v4.7.0/fonts/fontawesome-webfont.svg)
by Dave Gandy. The font is licensed under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/).
No complete font is distributed with this site.

Paper palette: Reading `#FFBF00`, Writing `#06B0F0`, Sharing `#82F5B1`,
Interacting `#CD93E3`. Symbol inks follow the manuscript: `#C08000`, `#0097CF`,
`#00A344`, and `#C363E9`. Small web text uses darker related colors for legibility.

## Design references

[OmniScientist](https://omni-scientist.github.io/) informs the centered title,
figure-led research presentation, and chapter progress navigation.
[Physical2RSI](https://eurekaleo.github.io/Awesome-Physical2RSI/) informs the
survey-to-reference-library flow and the use of original paper figures as the
main narrative. Their artwork and branding are not redistributed.
